Title: Standardized Precipitation Index User Guide (WMO-No. 1090)
Author/publisher: World Meteorological Organization, 2012
URL: https://www.droughtmanagement.info/literature/WMO_standardized_precipitation_index_user_guide_en_2012.pdf
Opened: 14 Nov 2026 by O1 (curl + PyMuPDF text extraction). Used for D42: drought compared with the same calendar month.

--- PDF page 13, "1-month SPI" ---
"For example, a 1-month SPI at the end of November compares the 1-month precipitation total for November in
that particular year with the November precipitation totals of all the years on record."
"Because the 1-month SPI reflects short-term conditions, its application can be related closely to
meteorological types of drought along with short-term soil moisture and crop stress, especially during the
growing season."

--- PDF page 13, "3-month SPI" ---
"The 3-month SPI provides a comparison of the precipitation over a specific 3-month period with the
precipitation totals from the same 3-month period for all the years included in the historical record."
