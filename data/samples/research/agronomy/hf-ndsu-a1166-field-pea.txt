Quoted text:
"Field pea is sensitive to heat stress at flowering, which can reduce pod and seed set."
"Seedlings are tolerant of spring frosts in the low 20s, and if injured by frost, a new shoot will emerge from below the soil surface until approximately seven nodes are above the soil surface."
"Flowering usually begins 40 to 50 days after planting. Flower duration is normally two to four weeks"
"Seeding field pea beyond mid-May will result in the crop beginning to flower in mid-July, which increases the risk of heat stress and disease problems" (North Dakota)
