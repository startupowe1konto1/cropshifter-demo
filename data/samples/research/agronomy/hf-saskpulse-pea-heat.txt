Title: Pea yield formation in warming temperatures - phenological mechanisms (research project summary)

Locator: "Project Description" and "Outcome" sections

Quoted text:
"Pea crops are sensitive to heat stress. A few days of heat above 29°C in the field causes flower and young pod abortion and lowers yield."
"We found that pea varieties were all heat sensitive, but some performed better than others. About 20% of the PAM varieties could flower and set pods in 40°C heat."
