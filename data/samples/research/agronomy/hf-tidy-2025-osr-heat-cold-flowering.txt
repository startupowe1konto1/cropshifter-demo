Note: peer-reviewed, open access. Thresholds are literature summary (Angadi et al. 2000; Elferjani & Soolanayakanahally 2018; Lohani et al. 2022), not a Polish recommendation. No frost-at-flowering number in °C.

"Chilling stress during flowering can induce flower abortion, pollen and ovule infertility [...] leading to lower yields"
"Early morning frosts and cold snaps can damage both vegetative and reproductive OSR organs"
"OSR is also extremely sensitive to heat stress, particularly during the reproductive stages"
"every 1 ◦C temperature increase from the optimum causes a 10 % yield reduction in OSR (Nuttall et al., 1992)"
"Various studies have indicated that high-temperature stress can occur at >25 ◦C in OSR, with >29 ◦C leading to
>50 % reduction in seed yield (Angadi et al., 2000; Elferjani and Soolanayakanahally, 2018; Lohani et al., 2022)."
