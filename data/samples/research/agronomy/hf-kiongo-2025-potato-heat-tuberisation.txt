Note: literature summary (Van Dam 1996; Dahal 2019; Tang 2018; Lafta & Lorenzen 1995; Rykaczewska 2015 [IHAR]).

"Potato [...] is regarded as a temperate crop with optimal above-ground growth at 20 to 25 ℃ and optimal
below-ground growth at 15 to 20 ℃ (Van Dam et al. 1996; Hastilestari et al. 2018)."
"Temperatures above 25 ℃ have been found to delay tuberisation in potato (Dahal et al. 2019) and higher
temperatures (35 ℃ day and 28 ℃ night) may completely inhibit tuberisation in some cultivars (Tang et al. 2018)."
"Lafta and Lorenzen (1995) found that at 31/29 ℃ day/night temperature, more dry matter was allocated to the
shoots at the expense of tuber growth."
"[...] the sensitivity of potato to high temperatures also depends on the duration of exposure (Ahn et al. 2004)
and the growth stage, with the tuberisation stage being the most sensitive (Rykaczewska 2015; Tang et al. 2018)."
