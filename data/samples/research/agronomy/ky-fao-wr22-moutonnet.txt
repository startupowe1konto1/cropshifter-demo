Treatment codes (quote):
"(a) initial (planting to 10 percent ground cover); (b) crop development (10 percent ground cover to effective full cover and initiation of flowering); (c) mid-season (effective soil cover to onset of maturity); (d) late season (onset of maturity to harvest). The deficit irrigation was applied only during one specific growth stage to be assessed for water sensitivity. The codes for these treatments were Tr.0111, Tr.1011, Tr.1101 and Tr.1110 (i.e.0 and 1 correspond to the stages during which irrigation water was or was not restricted, respectively)."
