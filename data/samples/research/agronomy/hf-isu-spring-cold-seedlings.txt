"Below is a National Weather Service low temperature prediction ( ) for Saturday, May 9."
"Temperature 28 oF or lower for a couple of hours will be lethal to growing tissue that has emerged. Temperatures between 28 oF and 32 oF will result in minimal frost injury to above ground tissue. And temperatures between 32 oF and 50 oF could result in poor vigor and growth."
"A dead growing point is unlikely unless temperatures get 28°F or lower for several hours." (corn)
"Soybean cotyledons are resilient to cold temperatures. Expect some frost damage to cotyledons and hypocotyl around the margins but survival is likely unless there were several hours of temperatures below 30 oF. The apical meristem and/or auxiliary buds are not expected to be killed at temperatures down to 28 oF."
