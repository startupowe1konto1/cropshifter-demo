"RECOMMENDED COVER CROP SYSTEMS PRECEDING SOYBEAN CASH CROP:"
"Drill after harvest or broadcast interseed at the corn dough (R4) stage."
"CAUTION against broadcast interseeding if the weather outlook is hot and dry; instead drill post-harvest."
"Allow the cover crop to reach a minimum of 8–12 inches before terminating."
"RECOMMENDED COVER CROP SYSTEMS PRECEDING CORN CASH CROP:"
"Drill after harvest or broadcast interseed near soybean leaf drop stage."
"Allow the cover crop to reach a minimum of 8–12 inches before chemical termination."
"An effective termination requires spraying when the cover crop is actively growing. Ideal conditions are consecutive
daytime temperatures above 60° F and nighttime lows above 40° F."
"roller crimping a plant with the growing point well above the soil surface, like cereal rye at anthesis (flowering) or later."
EN note: 60 °F = 15.5 °C, 40 °F = 4.5 °C; 8-12 inches = 20-30 cm.
