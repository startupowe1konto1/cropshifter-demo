Treatments: "(a) irrigated at 30 mm water deficit (fully irrigated); (b) a 50 mm water deficit imposed by polythene shelters during early grain-filling; (c) irrigation at 50 mm deficit if reached naturally."
Eqn 2 (Doorenbos and Kassam, 1979): "1- Ya/Ym = ky(P) x (1 - ETa/ETm) ... relates relative yield decrease ... to relative evapotranspiration deficit ... in the growth phase (P) by the drought sensitivity factor ky."
