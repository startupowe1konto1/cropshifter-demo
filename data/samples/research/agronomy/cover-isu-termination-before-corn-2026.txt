Title: Terminating cover crops ahead of corn
Author/publisher: Alison Robertson, Mark Licht; Iowa State University Extension and Outreach, Integrated Crop Management blog
Year: 2026 (published 22 Apr 2026)
URL: https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/post/terminating-cover-crops-ahead-corn
Date opened: 14 Nov 2026 (via oc CLI)
Locator: intro paragraph; Figure 1 caption; "Corn seedling disease" paragraph; "Recommendations" section

"We normally recommend terminating CCs with a herbicide application at least 10 days before planting corn"
Fig. 1: "winter cereal rye (WCR) terminated with glyphosate at >14 days before planting (planting brown), 3-7 days before
planting (planting yellow-brown) and 0-3 days after planting corn (planting green)."
"In 2021, 2022 and 2023, the relative yield of corn planted green was lower than the yield of corn planted brown at 10 of 13,
11 of 14, and 10 of 12 locations"
"If no host, like corn, is present, the population [Pythium] decreases to endemic levels within 7 to 10 days. However, if corn
is planted within 10 days of applying herbicide to WCR to terminate the cover crop, it is at greater risk for seedling disease"
"We recommended terminating a CC at least 10 days before planting corn. However, if this cannot be done, planting into warm
soils (>60°F) will encourage rapid emergence and reduce the risk of infection by seedling pathogens."
EN note: 60 °F = 15.5 °C.
