Title: How Do High Temperatures Impact My Soybeans?
Author/publisher: NC State Extension (Soybeans portal), North Carolina State University
Year: July 2020 (article text: "Now it is July"; 2020 season)
URL: https://soybeans.ces.ncsu.edu/news/how-do-high-temperatures-impact-my-soybeans/
Date opened: 14 Nov 2026 (read with the oc CLI)
Locator: paragraphs 2-4

Quoted text:
"The ideal temperature for soybean growth is 85℉ . When temperatures exceed this threshold, especially for several subsequent days, heat stress can occur."
"Temperatures above 86℉ can also reduce nodulation, and ultimately N-fixation in soybeans"
"High temperatures can result in any of the following in reproductive stage soybeans: aborted flowers, aborted small pods, aborted seeds in larger pods, and production of smaller seeds."
"Heat stress has the most adverse impact on soybeans in the R5 (beginning seed) growth stage."
Note: North Carolina, not Iowa. 85°F is described as the optimum above which stress can occur, not a damage threshold.
