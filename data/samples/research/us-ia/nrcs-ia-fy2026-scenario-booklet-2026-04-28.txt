Scenario Booklet Glossary
Practice Code Table - The table of Practices are in alphabetical order. The practice code for each has a hot link to the individual Practice Code
Scenario Descriptions.
Limitations
- This area will list any limitations a conservation practice may have related to EQIP, CSP, and RCPP18, i.e., payment limitations,
planning considerations, practice requirements, etc.
Maintenance - This is the Conservation Practice Lifespan.
Payment Schedule Headers:
ID - This is a numeric identifier for internal tracking purposes.
Scenario Name - Unique name for each scenario.
Scenario Description - For each Conservation Practice Scenario Name this column provides the Scenario Description.
Scenario Unit - Unit of measure for the scenario.
PAYMENT RATE – The payment rate is the amount of financial assistance ($/unit) available through EQIP and RCPP18.
Regular Rate – Payment rate is based on 75% of the estimated incurred costs and foregone income (if applicable)
associated with practice implementation.
HU, Wp, Pr Rate – Payment rate is based on 90% of the estimated incurred costs and foregone income (if applicable)
associated with practice implementation.
HU = Historically Underserved: Includes, Beginning Farmers/Ranchers, Limited Resource Farmers/Ranchers, Socially Disadvantaged
Farmers/Ranchers, Tribal Farmers/Ranchers and Veteran Farmers/Ranchers. The payment rate is higher for HU producers on most
practices.

CONSERVATION CROP ROTATION
Practice Code 328
Limitations:
• Scenario 328-104 Add Crop Organic System - Crop Type Examples: CSG (wheat, oats); CSB (camelina, radish); CSB Legumes (peas, lentils, clover);
WSG (corn, sorghum, millet); WSB (sunflowers, buckwheat); WSB Legumes (soybeans, sunnhemp)
• Scenario 328-1 Basic, Two Crop Types - Minimum two different crop types are required, No foregone income is included.
• Scenario 328-138 Perennial, Short-Term - Short-term perennial must be grown for minimum 2 years.
• Scenario 328-5 & Scenario 328-84 Specialty Crops - Specialty crops are typically rotated by plant family. Specialty crops include: vegetables, including
sweet corn and popcorn; fruit; tree fruit/nuts; herbs; other horticulture and nursery crops, including cut flowers. (i.e. crops other than grain corn,
soybeans, hay/alfalfa, oilseeds, cereal/small grains, dry beans, sugarbeets). The planned sequence may or may not allow for successional
plantings. Applicable to organic and non-organic production.
o Scenario 328-5 Specialty crops with a minimum of two different crops on the same ground over time. Typically multiple acres of mechanized,
commercial production.
o Scenario 328-84 Small scale or diversified small farm, growing specialty crops, with a minimum of two different specialty crop types.
• Scenario 328-139 Winter or Spring Annual - Must Add Winter or Spring Annuals or small grain to a crop rotation, minimum two crop types are required.
• The Conservation Crop Rotation practice may be paid annually for up to 5 consecutive years. The new crop rotation must be applied during the contract
period.
Maintenance: Practice will be maintained for a lifespan of 1 year.

COVER CROP
Practice 340
Limitations:
• Conservation Practice 340 Cover Crops is a management practice (One-year lifespan) and may be scheduled for up to 5 years on the same land unit.
• After cover crops are implemented, full-width tillage is not allowed until before planting of the next cash crop.
• For Scenario 6 - Adaptive Management, refer to Agronomy Technical Note No. 10: Adaptive Management for Conservation Practices and Adaptive Mgt 340
Guide Sheet 2014. All Adaptive Management proposals must be approved by Iowa NRCS State Soil Health Specialist prior to obligation.
• Scenario 340-74 Mechanical Termination - This scenario is most applicable to small-scale production. Termination methods may vary, but are labor
intensive, done at a human-scale (using hands, hand tools, garden implements). This scenario could apply to chemical termination when the spraying is
done by hand/backpack and when subsequent residue management is being done by hand, hand tools, or garden implements.
This is typically not appropriate for large fields >2 acres or where machinery will be used.
• Multi-Species - Must contain ≥2 Species,
• Single Species, Organic - May be used for organic or transitioning acres.
Maintenance: Cover Crop will be maintained for a lifespan of 1 year.
